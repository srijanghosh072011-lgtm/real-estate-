The kind of downtown house people wait two years for. The main floor kept its 1926 bones — heart pine, picture rails, a working brick fireplace — but everything behind the walls is new: full rewire, repiped supply lines, and a 16 SEER heat pump installed in 2023.

The carriage house out back is the part that changes the math. It is permitted, separately metered, and currently leased at $1,450 a month to a long-term tenant who would happily stay.

### What to know before you tour

Off-street parking is two cars in the rear alley, which is generous for this block. The crawlspace was encapsulated in 2022 and the report is in the disclosure packet. Flood zone X — no lender-required flood insurance.
