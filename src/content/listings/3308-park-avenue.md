A tidy 1958 ranch that has been in one family since 1974. It needs cosmetics, not systems — the roof is 2021, the HVAC is 2019, and the electrical panel was replaced when the workshop got its 240v run.

Pull the carpet in the bedrooms before you do anything else. There is original oak under all three, and we checked.

### What to know before you tour

Priced for the kitchen, which is original. Comparable renovated ranches on this street closed between $505k and $540k this year.
