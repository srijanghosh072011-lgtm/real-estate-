Deep water on Bradley Creek with six feet at mean low tide, which is the number that matters if you own anything bigger than a center console. The dock carries a 10,000 lb lift and a floating platform that was rebuilt after Florence.

Inside, the house reads calmer than its square footage suggests: one long living wall of impact-rated glass, a white oak kitchen, and a primary suite on the main floor so the upstairs can belong to guests.

### What to know before you tour

Elevated construction with the finished floor above base flood elevation, so flood premiums here run well below what buyers expect for a creek-front address. Bring the elevation certificate to your insurance agent before you write.
