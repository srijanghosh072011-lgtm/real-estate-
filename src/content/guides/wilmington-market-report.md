## The short version

Entry-level is tight, luxury is soft, and the middle is finally behaving like a normal market again.

## By the numbers

Median sale price across New Hanover County is up about 3.9% year over year. Months of supply sits near 3.8 county-wide — but that average hides the real story: under $500,000 supply is below 2.5 months and homes are still seeing competing offers, while above $1 million supply is past six months and sellers are negotiating.

Median days on market has stretched to roughly 31 days county-wide, up from 24 a year ago. Our own listings averaged 12, which is less about market conditions than about launching at a defensible price with the prep already done.

## What it means if you are selling

The buyer pool still exists, but it is patient. Overpricing costs more than it did in 2022 because there is enough inventory for buyers to simply wait. Price into the band, prep before launch, and expect to be under contract inside three weeks.

## What it means if you are buying

Above $800,000, ask for things. Rate buydowns, closing cost credits and repair credits are all being granted again in that band. Below $500,000, move fast and lead with terms — a short due diligence period and a larger non-refundable fee beat an extra $5,000 in price more often than not.
