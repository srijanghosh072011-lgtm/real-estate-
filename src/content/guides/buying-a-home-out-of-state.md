A third of our buyers purchase from out of state, and the ones who do it well follow roughly the same sequence.

## Before you write anything

**Get an insurance quote first.** On the coast this is the single largest variable in your monthly payment and it can differ by thousands between two houses on the same street. Send us the address and we will have a quote back the same day.

**Understand due diligence.** North Carolina does not use the inspection contingency you may know from other states. You buy a window of time with a non-refundable fee, and inside that window you can walk for any reason. Outside it, you generally cannot.

## The walkthrough video that matters

Listing photography is marketing. What you need is a phone video with narration that points a camera at the things photos are framed to avoid: the crawlspace access, the electrical panel, the roof line from the back corner, the soft spot in the sunroom floor. We shoot these the same day a property hits our shortlist.

## Closing without travel

A mobile notary comes to you, wherever you are. Wire instructions come by phone confirmation, never by email alone — wire fraud in real estate closings is common enough that we treat every emailed instruction as fraudulent until verified by voice with a number you looked up yourself.
