Income projections sell properties. Verified income buys them. The difference is about four phone calls.

## Verify the permit, not the claim

Ask the town's planning department directly whether the registration transfers on sale, and get the answer in writing. A listing that advertises "STR permitted" is describing the seller's status, not necessarily yours.

## Demand the actual records

Not a projection, not a comparable-property estimate from a data service — the seller's own booking history and payout statements for the last two full seasons. If they will not produce them, the projection is a guess and you should price it as one.

## Model the fixed costs honestly

Insurance, flood, HOA, management, cleaning turns, utilities, and a maintenance reserve that reflects salt air. Coastal properties consume exterior maintenance at a rate inland investors underestimate by roughly double.

## The occupancy cap detail

Several coastal municipalities cap occupancy by bedroom count and enforce parking minimums. A four-bedroom that sleeps twelve on paper may legally sleep eight, and that difference reshapes your nightly rate and your entire return model.
