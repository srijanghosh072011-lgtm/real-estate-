Most of the inventory here is under twelve years old, so the inspection conversation is shorter and the insurance is cheaper. The trade is HOA dues and a bridge.

### New build vs resale

Builder inventory carries incentives that resale cannot match — rate buydowns and closing cost credits worth $15,000 to $25,000 in the current cycle. Resale carries finished landscaping, blinds, screened porches and a fence, which together cost more than buyers expect. We run both numbers before you choose a lane.

### The commute

Twenty minutes to downtown outside peak, closer to thirty-five at 5pm on the bridge. Drive it once at the hour you would actually drive it.
