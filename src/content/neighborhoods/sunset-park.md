The fastest-moving price band in the county sits here, and it moves because renovated ranches close $80,000 to $100,000 above unrenovated ones on the same street. That gap is the whole investment thesis.

### Where the value hides

Carpet over original oak in bedrooms is common in 1950s builds. So are original cast-iron drain lines, which is the one plumbing item worth scoping before you close — a camera inspection costs about $250 and occasionally saves $9,000.

### Sellers

Homes here sell in a median of 14 days. Anything sitting past 30 is a pricing problem, not a market problem.
