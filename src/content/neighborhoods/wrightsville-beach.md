Price per square foot here is roughly double the mainland, and the spread between an ocean-side and a sound-side address on the same street is real money. Sound side buys you deep water and protection; ocean side buys you the walk.

### The number most buyers miss

Elevation certificate first, insurance quote second, offer third. Two otherwise identical houses can differ by $6,000 a year in flood premium based on finished floor height. We get quotes before writing, not after inspection.

### Rental rules

Short-term rentals are permitted with registration, but occupancy caps and parking minimums are enforced. If the income projection is part of your purchase math, we verify the permit and the last two years of actual booking records before due diligence ends.
