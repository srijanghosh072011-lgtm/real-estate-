Downtown is the one Wilmington submarket where condition varies more than location. Two houses on the same block can be $200,000 apart because one has a 1970s roof deck and a crawlspace full of moisture and the other was fully restored in 2019.

### What buyers should check first

Anything inside the historic district overlay needs Historic Preservation Commission approval for exterior changes — including windows, which is where most renovation budgets go sideways. Ask for the certificate of appropriateness history before you write an offer.

### What sellers should know

Buyers here read disclosure packets closely. A pre-list inspection and a repaired punch list typically returns three to four times its cost, because it removes the negotiation lever that downtown buyers expect to pull.
