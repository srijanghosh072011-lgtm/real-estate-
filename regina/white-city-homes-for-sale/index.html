About White City & Emerald Park homes for sale

The value case, in actual numbers

Median's around $487,000, which out here buys 2,000 to 2,400 square feet on a quarter acre or more, often with a heated triple garage. The same lot and garage inside Regina, if you could even find it, would be well past $600,000. Taxes are lower with no city mill rate, though you'll want to confirm the current RM of Edenwold levy for the specific parcel.

Ask about water and septic before anything else

Don't assume services. Some properties run municipal water and sewer, some a drilled well with treatment, some a cistern with hauled water. Septic arrangements vary just as much. Each carries a different maintenance cost and a different effect on financing and insurance. Sort out which one you're buying before the offer, not during the inspection.

The second vehicle nobody budgets for

This is the part people miss. Out here every errand is a highway trip and almost every household runs two vehicles. Fuel, insurance, maintenance, depreciation. A good chunk of what you saved on the house goes straight back out. Families who move for the space rarely regret it. Couples who moved purely on price per square foot sometimes do.

Schools and the bus

White City School serves the community and older students get bussed. For young kids the ride is short and uneventful. For teenagers with activities in the city, you become the transport, most evenings, through winter. Picture that concretely before you commit.

Questions

Questions people ask

Is White City cheaper than Regina?

Per square foot and per acre, clearly. Taxes are lower too without a city mill rate. Total cost of living is closer than the sticker price suggests once you count a second vehicle.

How far is White City from Regina?

Roughly 15 kilometres east on Highway 1. About fifteen minutes to the Ring Road, twenty to twenty-five to downtown outside of winter conditions.

Do White City homes have city water?

Many do. White City runs municipal water and sewer in the developed subdivisions. Acreage parcels outside them often rely on a well or cistern with a septic field, so always check the specific property.
