Harbour Landing homes for sale

Harbour Landing is the biggest planned neighbourhood Regina's built in fifty years, and almost every house in it went up after 2008. That one fact explains most of what it's like to live here. Maintenance you can predict. Modern windows. Insurance quotes that don't flinch. A street grid built around a grocery store instead of a grain elevator.

It's also the neighbourhood buyers fall for on a Saturday and second-guess by Tuesday, because the lots are small and the boulevards have no shade yet. So here's what we tell people before they write.

About Harbour Landing homes for sale

What the money actually buys

Median's around $412,000. That gets you 1,800 to 2,200 square feet, three or four bedrooms, an attached double garage, and a basement that's either finished or ready to be. Cathedral gives you more house and more land for the same money, but it comes with 1920s wiring attached. Here you're paying not to think about the house. For plenty of people that's worth it.

Schools, and the walk to them

Dr. George Ferguson and Harbour Landing School are both inside the neighbourhood, which is why families with young kids dominate the buyer pool. Anything within a real walking radius of either one holds its value better and sells faster in spring. One warning: the catchment boundaries cut right through the neighbourhood, so check the specific address with Regina Public Schools. Don't trust the name on the sign.

The part that catches people out

Lots are narrow, often 35 to 42 feet, and the houses sit close. If you want a yard your kids can play soccer in, or a garage deep enough for a truck and a workbench, you'll be happier in White City or on an older Lakeview lot. And the tree cover is twenty years out. In August this is a bright, hot, wide-open place. It isn't shady.

Suites are worth searching for specifically

A lot of the newer builds here were framed with a separate basement entrance, and legal suites are common enough to hunt for on purpose. A one-bedroom suite in Harbour Landing rents for roughly $1,100 to $1,250 with utilities. For a lot of buyers that's the difference between qualifying and not. Which is exactly why suited houses here go fast and almost never need a price drop.

Questions

Questions people ask

Is Harbour Landing a good place to buy in Regina?

If you want a newer, low-maintenance house with schools and groceries you can walk to, it's probably the best choice in the city. If you want land, big trees, character or a workshop, it's the wrong neighbourhood and no amount of showings will fix that.

How much is a house in Harbour Landing?

Median sale price sits around $412,000 over a rolling year. Townhouses and condos start near $250,000. The bigger executive builds on the southern edge run into the $600,000s.

Are Harbour Landing homes new enough to skip an inspection?

No. We've found bad lot grading, failing sumps and rough basement developments in houses under ten years old. New construction changes which problems you're looking for. It doesn't remove them. Inspect anyway.
