Story: The Shinjuku Showdown

December 24th, 2018. Play as each sorcerer who stood against Sukuna, in the order they fought him. If you lose, you see what really happened in the manga. If you win, you see a "what if" ending.

How to Play

W A S D / arrowsMove. The camera stays side-on, like a 3D fighting game. W/S steps into or out of the screen.
Space / CFly up / fly down. Every sorcerer here can fight in the air.
JStrike. Three-hit combo, and it lunges toward the enemy if they're close.
K LCursed techniques. Each costs cursed energy (purple bar).
IUltimate technique. Charged beams can be sidestepped, and hitting the caster cancels them.
ODomain Expansion. Needs a full gold meter, which fills when you deal or take damage.
E / QDash (short invincibility) / hold to guard (blocks 75%).
EscPause. In cutscenes, Enter, Space or a click advances.

Lore mechanics: Gojo's Infinity blocks most damage until Mahoraga adapts. Yuji and Todo can land Black Flash. Maki has no cursed energy, so domains can't lock onto her. Higuruma's domain confiscates techniques, and a confiscated target is open to the Executioner's Sword.
