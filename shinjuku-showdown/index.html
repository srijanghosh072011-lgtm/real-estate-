Story Mode

Each chapter plays one fight from the story, in order. When the canon result is a loss, losing shows you what really happened and winning gives you a "what if".

How to Play

W A S D / arrowsMove. The camera stays side-on, like a 3D fighting game. W/S steps into or out of the screen.
Space / CFly up / down. Stand on rooftops, fly over buildings, fight above the skyline.
JStrike: jab, cross, kick. The third hit launches.
K L ICursed techniques and your ultimate. Heavy hits send the enemy flying through buildings.
EDash. When the enemy is far away or flying, it becomes a chase that closes the gap instantly.
ODomain Expansion (full gold meter). If both expand, it becomes a Domain Clash: mash O/J. Afterwards your technique burns out briefly.
USummon: Sukuna calls Mahoraga, Yuta manifests Rika, Geto calls Kuchisake-onna.
Q / EscHold to guard / pause. The Exit button at the top of the screen pauses with Restart and Quit. In cutscenes, Enter or a click advances, and Exit returns to the title.

The real map: the fight started on Nishi-Shinjuku's Chūō-dōri and ended near the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building. The map runs from Shinjuku Central Park through Nishi-Shinjuku's towers, across the station's rail yard, to Kabukichō, Golden Gai and Shinjuku Gyoen. The minimap shows where you are. Season 0 is fought at Tokyo Jujutsu High: timber halls, a five-storey pagoda and cedar woods in the hills.

Lore mechanics: Gojo's Infinity blocks most damage. Every blow it blocks turns Mahoraga's wheel, and after four turns Sukuna can cut through space itself. Maki is invisible to domains. Higuruma's verdict confiscates techniques.

Sound & Voice

The voices come from Kokoro, an open-source neural speech model. The Japanese track reads a Japanese script, like watching the anime with subtitles; the English track reads the subtitles. Each character gets their own voice, pitch and effects. They are not the anime's voice actors and are not imitations of them.
