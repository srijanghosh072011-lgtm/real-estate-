Seller questions

How much does it cost to sell my home with Everline?

A published commission schedule, given to you in writing before you sign anything, including exactly what the co-operating brokerage receives. Professional photography, floor plans, staging consultation and the marketing budget are included, not billed back.

How accurate is the online home valuation?

The instant estimate is a starting range built from recent comparable sales in your neighbourhood. It cannot see your renovations, your lot, or your condition. Treat it as a bracket, then have us walk the property for a real number.
